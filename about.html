About Us

Care that listens before it treats.

AUDIOMIND SPEECH AND HEARING LLP was founded to close the gap between ENT medicine and audiology — so a patient never has to explain their story twice.

Our approach

One team, one file, one plan

Hearing and speech concerns rarely have a single cause. A child who isn't speaking on time may have a mild hearing loss behind it; an adult's tinnitus may trace back to an ear infection. That's why every AUDIOMIND branch keeps the ENT doctor and the audiologist working from the same patient file — test results, consultation notes and therapy progress in one place.

We built AUDIOMIND around three branches — Rewari, Bhiwadi and Bareilly — so families do not have to travel far for consistent, follow-up care.

What guides us

Our commitments

  • Every hearing aid is fitted only after a proper diagnostic test — never sold off a guess.
  • Every child referred for speech delay is first checked for hearing loss.
  • Every consultation is explained in plain language, with the audiogram shown and read out to the patient.
  • Every branch follows the same protocols, so care is consistent wherever you visit.
