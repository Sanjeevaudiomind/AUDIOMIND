Services & Tests

What actually happens at each visit.

Every service below notes what it involves, and whether it's led by our ENT doctor or our audiologist.

Doctor-led

ENT Consultation

A full check of the ear, nose and throat by Dr. Manish Yadav to find the medical cause behind pain, discharge, blocked hearing or recurring infection.

  • Otoscopic examination of the ear canal and eardrum
  • Diagnosis of ear infection, perforation or fluid build-up
  • Nose and throat examination where relevant (allergy, sinus, voice issues)
  • Medical clearance before any hearing aid trial
Doctor-led

Ear Wax & Minor Procedures

Safe removal of impacted ear wax and minor in-clinic procedures that can be causing muffled hearing or discomfort.

  • Microscope-guided ear wax removal (syringing or suction)
  • Foreign body removal from the ear canal
  • Dressing and follow-up for minor ear infections
Audiologist-led

Hearing Tests (Audiometry)

Diagnostic tests that measure exactly how well you hear, at every frequency, and plot it on an audiogram.

  • Pure Tone Audiometry (PTA) — measures the softest sound you can hear at different pitches
  • Tympanometry — checks how well the eardrum moves and whether there's fluid behind it
  • OAE (Otoacoustic Emissions) — tests inner-ear (cochlea) function, commonly used for newborns
  • BERA (Brainstem Evoked Response Audiometry) — checks how sound signals travel from the ear to the brain, used for infants and difficult-to-test cases
  • Speech Audiometry — checks how clearly you understand speech, not just tones
Audiologist-led

Hearing Aid Trial & Fitting

Once the audiogram is ready, our audiologist recommends and fits devices matched to your hearing loss and lifestyle.

  • Device selection across budgets (BTE, RIC, ITE and invisible options)
  • On-ear trial before purchase
  • Digital programming matched precisely to the audiogram
  • Real-ear verification to confirm the fitting is accurate
  • Free follow-up adjustments and cleaning during the warranty period
Audiologist-led

Speech & Language Therapy

One-to-one sessions for children and adults working on how sounds, words and language are produced and understood.

  • Articulation therapy for unclear speech sounds
  • Language stimulation for late talkers and vocabulary delay
  • Stammering (fluency) therapy
  • Voice therapy for hoarseness or vocal strain
  • Auditory-verbal therapy for children using hearing aids or cochlear implants
Audiologist-led

Pediatric Hearing & Speech Screening

Early screening so hearing or speech delays are caught and treated before they affect schooling.

  • Newborn and infant hearing screening (OAE/BERA)
  • Speech-milestone checks by age
  • School-readiness hearing and language screening
  • Referral to ENT if a medical cause is suspected
Doctor + Audiologist

Tinnitus Management

For ringing, buzzing or whistling sounds in the ear — assessed medically first, then managed jointly.

  • ENT check to rule out a medical or infection-related cause
  • Hearing test to check for an underlying hearing loss
  • Counselling-based tinnitus management and sound-therapy guidance
  • Ongoing review of triggers and coping strategies
