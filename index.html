What we treat

ENT, hearing and speech — assessed and treated together

Our doctor and audiologists work as one team, so a hearing concern is never looked at in isolation from ear health, or from how a child is learning to speak.

01

ENT Consultation

Diagnosis and treatment of ear infections, wax build-up, nasal and throat conditions by our ENT specialist.

02

Hearing Tests

Pure tone audiometry, tympanometry, OAE and BERA testing to measure exactly how you hear, at every frequency.

03

Hearing Aid Fitting

Trial, selection and fine-programming of hearing aids matched to your audiogram and daily routine.

04

Speech Therapy

One-to-one therapy for children and adults working on articulation, language delay, stammering and voice.

05

Pediatric Screening

Early hearing and speech-milestone screening for infants and young children, with school-readiness checks.

06

Tinnitus Management

Assessment and counselling-based management for ringing, buzzing or whistling sounds in the ear.

Two specialists, one plan

Who you'll meet — and why both matter

Doctor (ENT Specialist)

Handles the medical side of ear, nose and throat health.

  • Examines the ear canal and eardrum for infection, wax or damage
  • Diagnoses medical causes of hearing loss, pain or discharge
  • Prescribes medicine or minor procedures (e.g. safe wax removal)
  • Decides if a case needs surgery and refers accordingly
  • Clears patients medically before hearing aid fitting

Audiologist

Handles measurement, hearing devices and rehabilitation.

  • Conducts hearing tests and plots the audiogram
  • Recommends, trials and programs hearing aids
  • Runs speech and language therapy sessions
  • Screens infants and children for hearing/speech delay
  • Guides tinnitus management and follow-up care
